## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Little Maker STEAM: Early Math & Science Excellence

The Heising-Simons Foundation's commitment to early math and science 
education directly aligns with our signature curriculum. The Little Maker 
Program is a 26-week, A-Z STEAM curriculum that provides hands-on science, 
technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics instruction to children 
from Pre-K through After School ages.

**Key Math/Science Components:**
- Measurement and data collection (Kitchen & Kindness, Week K)
- Pattern recognition and mathematical reasoning (Botany & Building, Week B)
- Scientific observation and recording (Oceanography & Observation, Week O)
- Engineering design challenges (Economics & Engineering, Week E)
- Earth science exploration (Geology & Gardening, Week G)
- Physics concepts through play (Ceramics & Construction, Week C)
- Technology integration (Robotics, After School Program)

**Two-Year Plan:**

**Year 1:** Curriculum Enhancement & Educator Training
- Train all educators in evidence-based early math/science pedagogy
- Develop supplemental math/science learning materials
- Integrate assessment tools to track math/science skill development
- Host educator learning community (monthly peer sessions)
- Partner with STEM professionals for monthly classroom visits

**Year 2:** Program Evaluation & Knowledge Sharing
- Implement pre/post assessments for math/science readiness
- Document curriculum effectiveness and best practices
- Present findings at early childhood education conferences
- Create open-source curriculum guide for other underserved programs
- Transition to sustained local funding model
