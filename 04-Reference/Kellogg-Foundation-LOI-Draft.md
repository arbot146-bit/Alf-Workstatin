## ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW

Legacy First Foundation is a confirmed 501(c)(3) nonprofit and Texas-registered 
organization operating Little Legacy Academy, a licensed STEAM+Montessori 
childcare facility in Houston's Settegast community. Founded by Misha M. 
Slaughter, the academy serves families in one of Houston's historically 
underserved neighborhoods — TX-18 congressional district.

**Mission:** To nurture confident, kind, and capable children through a hands-on 
early learning experience that honors family, community, and the unique gifts 
of every child.

**What Makes Us Unique:** Our signature 26-Week Little Maker Program delivers 
comprehensive A-Z STEAM curriculum (Apothecary through Zoology) to children 
from 18 months through 12 years — a critical gap in a community where quality 
early childhood education options are severely limited.

---

## COMMUNITY NEED

The Settegast neighborhood of Houston, located in TX-18 and Harris County, 
faces significant barriers to educational equity:

- **Limited access to quality childcare:** The nearest high-quality early 
  learning centers require long commutes from Settegast, placing them out 
  of reach for many working families.
- **Income disparity:** A significant percentage of Settegast families are 
  low-income and lack access to affordable, high-quality early education.
- **Educational gap:** Children in underserved neighborhoods often enter 
  kindergarten behind their peers — a gap that compounds over time.
- **Workforce impact:** Without reliable, quality childcare, parents in the 
  Settegast community face barriers to employment and career advancement.

Little Legacy Academy addresses this gap by offering premium STEAM and 
Montessori-inspired education with extended hours (6:30 AM – 6:30 PM), 
sliding-scale tuition, and a deep commitment to the whole child.

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### The Little Maker Program Expansion

With W.K. Kellogg Foundation support, Legacy First Foundation will expand 
access to our proven 26-Week Little Maker A-Z STEAM curriculum, serving 
more children in the Settegast community and deepening program quality.

**Three-Year Goals:**

**Year 1 (2026-2027):** Program Expansion & Staff Development
- Hire and train 3 additional STEAM-certified educators
- Expand enrollment capacity by 40%
- Launch toddler STEAM track (18mo-2yr)
- Develop community parent-education workshops
- Establish baseline child outcome metrics

**Year 2 (2027-2028):** Deepening Impact & Community Integration
- Implement full A-Z STEAM curriculum across all age groups
- Launch after-school robotics and coding program (ages 5-12)
- Host quarterly community STEAM Family Nights
- Partner with 2 additional local organizations for wraparound services
- Conduct mid-program assessment and outcomes analysis

**Year 3 (2028-2029):** Sustainability & Replication
- Achieve 90%+ enrollment capacity
- Document and publish Little Maker curriculum framework (open-source)
- Train educators from 2 neighboring communities in our model
- Establish endowment fund for sliding-scale tuition sustainability
- Final impact assessment and longitudinal outcomes report

**Curriculum Highlights (Little Maker A-Z):**

D — Design & Decor | E — Economics & Engineering | F — Fashion & Fabric
G — Geology & Gardening | H — Hospitality & Home | I — Interior Arts & Illustration
J — Jewelry & Joining | K — Kitchen & Kindness | L — Language & Literature
M — Music & Movement | N — Nature & Navigation | O — Oceanography & Observation

## ALIGNMENT WITH W.K. KELLOGG FOUNDATION PRIORITIES

This proposal directly aligns with WKKF's commitment to:

1. **Equitable Education:** The Little Maker Program provides high-quality 
   early STEAM education to children in an underserved Black community, 
   addressing systemic educational inequity at its roots — the earliest years.

2. **Children & Families:** Serving children 18mo-12yr with programs that 
   strengthen family engagement through parent workshops and Community 
   STEAM Family Nights.

3. **Community-Engaged Approach:** Little Legacy Academy was founded in and 
   for the Settegast community. Our model is built on deep community 
   connections, cultural responsiveness, and honoring family values.

4. **Racial Equity:** Centering Black children and families in Settegast, 
   providing them with the same caliber of STEAM education available in 
   wealthier Houston neighborhoods.

5. **Thriving Children:** Every element of our program — from sensory play 
   for toddlers to robotics for pre-teens — is designed to help children 
   thrive cognitively, socially, and emotionally.
