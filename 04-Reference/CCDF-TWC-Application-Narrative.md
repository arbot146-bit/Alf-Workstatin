SECTION 2: PROGRAM DESCRIPTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Little Legacy Academy provides high-quality early childhood education 
for children ages 18 months through 12 years in the Settegast 
neighborhood of Houston, Texas.

**AGES SERVED:**

1. TODDLER (18 months – 2 years)
   - Gentle routines, sensory play, early language foundations
   - Practical-life and self-care skills
   - Small-group ratios for warm bonds
   - Montessori-inspired independence activities

2. PRESCHOOL (2 – 3 years)
   - Montessori independence activities
   - Pouring, scooping, pre-writing skills
   - Color, shape, number foundations
   - Beginning Little Maker STEAM projects

3. PRE-K (3 – 5 years)
   - Kindergarten readiness
   - Phonics, early reading, and writing
   - Math operations and patterning
   - 26-week Little Maker A-Z STEAM curriculum
   - Social-emotional and leadership skills

4. AFTER SCHOOL (5 – 12 years)
   - Homework support and tutoring
   - Maker electives: robotics, art, cooking
   - Healthy snacks and quiet zones

**HOURS OF OPERATION:** Monday–Friday, 6:30 AM – 6:30 PM
(Extended hours to accommodate working families)

SECTION 3: COMMUNITY NEED & TARGET POPULATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Little Legacy Academy serves the Settegast community — a historically 
underserved neighborhood in northeast Houston's TX-18 congressional 
district, Harris County.

**NEED ASSESSMENT:**

The Settegast community faces a critical shortage of quality early 
childhood education. Key challenges include:

1. **Provider scarcity:** Limited licensed childcare facilities within 
   walking or short-driving distance for Settegast families.

2. **Family income barrier:** Many families in the area are low-income 
   and cannot afford premium childcare options available in other 
   Houston neighborhoods. Without affordable quality care, parents 
   face barriers to employment and career stability.

3. **School readiness gap:** Children from underserved neighborhoods 
   often enter kindergarten behind their peers. Without early 
   intervention, this gap compounds through elementary school.

4. **Extended hours need:** Many Settegast families are working 
   parents who need care beyond standard 9-5 hours. Our 6:30 AM – 
   6:30 PM schedule directly serves working families.

**TARGET POPULATION:**
- 80%+ of enrolled families are low-to-moderate income
- Primary service area: Settegast and surrounding northeast Houston 
  neighborhoods (77028, 77026, 77078 zip codes)
- Racial/ethnic diversity reflective of the Settegast community
- Single-parent households and families receiving public assistance 
  are priority enrollment

SECTION 4: QUALITY IMPROVEMENT PLAN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Little Legacy Academy maintains the highest standards of quality:

1. **Evidence-Based Curriculum:** The 26-Week Little Maker Program 
   provides structured STEAM instruction aligned with Texas Pre-K 
   Guidelines and NAEYC standards.

2. **Qualified Educators:** All lead teachers hold or are pursuing 
   CDA credentials, Associate's, or Bachelor's degrees in early 
   childhood education.

3. **Small Class Sizes:** Maintaining low student-teacher ratios 
   to ensure individualized attention and strong relationships.

4. **Family Engagement:** Regular parent communication, quarterly 
   family events, and parent education workshops.

5. **Continuous Improvement:** Annual program evaluation using 
   authentic assessment tools, parent feedback, and outcome data.

6. **Professional Development:** Ongoing training in Montessori 
   methods, STEM education, trauma-informed care, and culturally 
   responsive teaching.
