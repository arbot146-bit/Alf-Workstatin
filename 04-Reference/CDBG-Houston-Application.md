SECTION 2: NATIONAL OBJECTIVE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This project meets the CDBG **Low/Moderate Income (LMI) Area Benefit** 
national objective by providing childcare services in the Settegast 
neighborhood, where the majority of residents are at or below 80% 
of Area Median Income.

**Census Tract Data:**
- Settegast is located in Houston's TX-18 District
- Harris County median income: ~$62,000
- Settegast area median income: approximately $25,000-$35,000
- Poverty rate: significantly above city average
- Confirmed LMI area — eligible for CDBG funding

SECTION 3: PROJECT DESCRIPTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Requested CDBG Funding: $200,000**

Little Legacy Academy proposes to use CDBG funds to improve and 
expand its childcare facility to better serve low-and-moderate 
income families in the Settegast neighborhood. The project includes:

**A. Facility Improvements (Priority)**
- HVAC system upgrade for health/safety compliance
- Playground construction with ADA-accessible equipment
- Safety improvements: fire suppression, security systems
- Outdoor learning garden (ties to STEAM curriculum)

**B. Capacity Expansion**
- Convert underutilized space to additional classroom
- Add toddler program space (currently waiting list)
- Expand after-school program area for 5-12yr olds

**C. Community Access**
- Multi-purpose room for parent education/workshops
- Family resource center with internet/computer access
- Community meeting space for neighborhood organizations
