SECTION 4: COMMUNITY NEED (Narrative for Applications)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Little Legacy Academy is located in the Settegast community of Houston, 
Harris County, Texas — a historically underserved neighborhood in the TX-18 
congressional district.

The Settegast community faces significant challenges in access to quality 
early childhood education. Many families in the area are low-income and lack 
access to affordable, high-quality childcare and preschool programs. The 
nearest quality early learning options are limited, forcing families to 
choose between long commutes or substandard care.

Little Legacy Academy addresses this gap by offering:
- Premium STEAM and Montessori-inspired education at accessible price points
- Sliding scale tuition / inquire for pricing and discounts
- Extended hours (6:30 AM – 6:30 PM) to accommodate working families
- A intentional focus on the whole child: cognitive, social-emotional, physical

Our signature 26-week Little Maker Program ensures every child, regardless 
of ZIP code, receives hands-on Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and 
Math instruction that builds kindergarten readiness and lifelong curiosity.
